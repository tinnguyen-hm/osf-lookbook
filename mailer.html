Frequently Asked Questions

Your Hike Medical custom insole benefit · OSF Employee Benefit
How It Works
1. You're Covered: OSF benefits provide one free pair of custom insoles to every Mission Partner.
2. Simple Process: Complete a quick five-minute phone scan and receive your covered 2026 Hike Insoles, custom designed for your unique foot shape, in two weeks. Amazing fit or Hike will rebuild for free.
3. Ongoing Support: After the first year, OSF benefits allow Mission Partners to purchase one replacement pair annually for just $15.
4. Want Additional Pairs Before 2027? That's easy from the member dashboard and exclusively priced for OSF Mission Partners.
What if my insoles don't fit?

That's where the Hike Fit Guarantee comes in. If it's not right, we'll figure out what's wrong and make you a new pair, on us.

Do I need a doctor's visit or prescription?

No. You order entirely from home with no appointments, referrals, or paperwork. All you need is your smartphone and about 5 minutes.

Is my information private during the scan?

Yes. The scan captures only the shape of your feet. No location data is recorded.

How do I place my order?

Scan the QR code on the front of this letter. Create your account, scan your feet in-app, and submit your order.

How long does delivery take?

Your custom insoles are typically delivered to the provided address within approximately 2 weeks of placing your order.

How do I scan my feet?

Using your smartphone, Hike technology captures the shape of your feet as you glide the phone in an arc around your foot. Scan the QR code to watch the how-to video.
