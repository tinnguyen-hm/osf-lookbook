Custom Insole Benefit

Provided by Hike Medical
Eligible
HOW IT WORKS
  1. You're Covered: OSF provides one free pair to every Mission Partner.
  2. Simple Process: complete a five-minute scan, receive your covered pair in 2 weeks.
  3. Ongoing Support: after year one, replacement pairs are $15 annually.
  4. Want More? Additional pairs available anytime from the member dashboard.
HOW TO REDEEM
Start your scan at hikemedical.com/osf from your phone.
DELIVERY TIME
Approximately 2 weeks from scan completion.
DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED
None. Eligibility is verified automatically through your OSF benefits enrollment.
BENEFIT PERIOD
Renews annually on your plan's benefit year start date.
CONTACT
support@hikemedical.com · Text/Call 1-309-306-3696
Eligibility to receive Hike Custom Insoles is subject to the agreement between your organization and Hike Medical, including your current employment and/or membership status. Questions? Contact your benefits administrator or support@hikemedical.com.
