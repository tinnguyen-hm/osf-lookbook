Custom Insoles

Provided by Hike Medical

OSF benefits includes custom insoles from Hike Medical — every pair, no doctor appointments, no paperwork.

Drugstore inserts are molded once, for an average foot, and sold to everyone. Custom insoles are built to match your exact foot shape, helping your body move the way it's meant to instead of pushing that strain up your skeleton.
